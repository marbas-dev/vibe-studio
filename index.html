Common questions

  • My progress disappeared. Game data is stored locally on your device; reinstalling or clearing the app removes it.
  • Is this online? The game is single-player and plays offline; it only connects to the internet to load ads.
  • Are there real purchases? No real-money purchases — gems are a free in-game currency. Ads are real: Google AdMob rewarded videos that you choose to watch for in-game bonuses.
